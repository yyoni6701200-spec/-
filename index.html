Frequently asked questions

Will these work on iPhone and Samsung Galaxy?

The artwork uses vertical proportions and can be set as a background on modern phones. Exact cropping depends on your device, wallpaper settings and screen ratio.

Is this a physical product?

No. It is intended to be a digital wallpaper collection; nothing will be shipped to your home.

How do I get my files?

Secure automatic delivery is planned, but the payment provider and download delivery must be configured before the site can take real orders.

Can I preview a wallpaper?

Yes. Use the “Preview” link beneath any design in the gallery to open the artwork.

What about refunds?

A clear digital-product refund policy must be finalized and reviewed for the laws that apply to the seller and customer before launch.
